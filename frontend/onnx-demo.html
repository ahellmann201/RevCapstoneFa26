ON DEVICE · ONNX

        
Check a frame and correct the lane

        

          This part runs the ONNX model locally on this device. Once the lane
          is set, ball detection is limited to its outline. Drag the four
          corners to correct the lane across the clip. If no lane is detected,
          select a frame and use “Set lane corners” to draw it manually; this
          also works when automatic ONNX inference is unavailable. The demo
          samples up to 60 frames and adjusts how often ONNX runs based on measured device speed.
